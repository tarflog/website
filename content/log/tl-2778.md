I went to an asteroid to collect a recorder. Two days, including the journey. I came back after eight. The recorder had caught stones leaving the surface. I checked the camera mount, checked the timestamps, then set up two more cameras. By the time I had packed, something else had moved. I unpacked. There was no communications failure. Each evening I meant to write here after checking one more sequence. I have several excellent sequences and an eight-day gap in the journal. The old recorder is now on my desk. There is a new one on the asteroid. It seemed wasteful to stop watching just because I had finally managed to leave.
