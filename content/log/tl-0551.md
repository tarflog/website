I found a complaint about an inn in an old notebook. The room was cold. The supper was late. Someone hammered above my head until morning. I described the proprietor as incapable of managing a quiet house. On the next page I thanked him for arranging the overnight construction of a workbench for my field station. I had insisted it be ready at dawn. The carpenter had worked in the room above mine because it was the only one large enough. I am transcribing both pages. The workbench served me well for years. I apparently found no time to put that in the review.
