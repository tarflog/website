In an old field recording, someone calls me to supper. I say I will be there in a moment. There is a long stretch of equipment noise. Then the voice comes back, closer to the microphone, and asks whether my moment is nearly finished. I hear myself giving an estimate. There is a laugh. The measurements from that evening have been in a table for years. I opened the recording to check a number, found it and left the file playing. At the end there is the sound of a chair moving. I did eventually go. I saved the whole recording in the new archive, including the wait.
