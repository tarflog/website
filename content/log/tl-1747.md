I lost the first game and suggested another. My opponent said he ought to be going. I said we had time for a quick one. I won that game. It was no longer particularly early, so I put my coat on. He began setting the pieces out again. I pointed at the clock. He repeated, very precisely, what I had said about having time for a quick one. We ordered more tea. Halfway through the third game he asked whether age had made me more patient. I asked him to make his move.
