Overnight, a field lies down in a pattern. The stalks are bent at the base, not broken, and the arcs are exact to the width of a stalk. You keep asking whether they are real. They are real. They are forms, filled out in the wrong field, and the Council returned every one of them the same way. In 1991 two of you confessed to making them with a plank and a rope. The confession was accepted. I have read it. It is also a form, correctly filled out, and it is filed with the others. The same line is struck out on all of them. Addressed to. I marked one stalk this morning. Received.
