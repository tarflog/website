topic: An old ransom receipt

My predecessor was describing the time he paid to get me released from custody. He remembers the negotiations in great detail. I remember being very pleased to see him. He then suggested that I had never repaid the money. I fetched the receipt. He had taken the sum from my travelling purse, paid it on my behalf and entered the transaction in his own accounts as a personal loan. He read the receipt twice. “You were still rescued,” he said. I agreed. He asked whether interest on the loan was at least open to discussion. I put the receipt back where I could find it easily.
