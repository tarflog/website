Biscuit’s stick went through the fence. I offered him another one. It was a better size and had no thorns. He looked at it, then looked through the fence. I opened a small passage, reached through and retrieved the original. He inspected it, carried it back to me and dropped it on my shoes. I moved farther from the fence before throwing it again. He brought it back immediately. We have now established what the afternoon is for.
