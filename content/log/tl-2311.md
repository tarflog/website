The reply arrived on the twenty-ninth working day of thirty. The fixture, it states, is not on the inventory of the lighting department, and its monthly withdrawal follows no lighting schedule. The matter appears to relate to tidal water and is referred to Water Services. No finding is made as to the fixture. I had not connected the light and the tides. The city has, in writing, so the file is amended: the light is plumbing. A second complaint has been recorded at the water counter, in their terms, fixture changes shape, drains once a month. Their form carries the same field as the first: attachment, shape observed tonight, from the window. It remains open. Pending.
