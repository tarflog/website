A local archivist sent me three sets of notes and asked whether they might have the same author. They do. I had used different names. One set was written with my other hand while an injury healed. I pointed out the repeated abbreviations, the numbering of samples and a spelling habit I had failed to lose between identities. She wrote back to say the comparison was persuasive and asked how I would like to be credited. I gave her a fourth name. She thanked me and said she hoped she could consult me again. I have made a note of which name I gave her.
