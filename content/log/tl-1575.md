Frank asked whether I had found what I went for. I said yes, and opened the pictures. He had pictures too. The roof had been leaking around a joint that looked perfectly sound from below. He had taken it apart, found the split and rebuilt the section. He showed me where the water had been getting in. We went upstairs so I could see the finished work. Then we came down and looked at the asteroid. Frank wanted to know how I had fixed the cameras to the surface. We spent most of the evening discussing fastenings. I had prepared a much more impressive account of my absence.
