The repairer turned my field bag inside out and stopped at an old seam. She said it must have been done in a hurry. I said I had had most of an afternoon. “Bad light?” “Quite good.” She looked at the stitching again. I asked her to leave that part and replace the torn panel beside it. She wanted to know whether the seam had any special significance. I told her it was the first repair I had made that had held. She gave it a firm pull. It held. The bag is back in use. Her new stitching is straight enough to make mine look worse from across the room.
