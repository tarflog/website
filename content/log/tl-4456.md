I went to the cafe intending to order something different. The cook had seen me coming down the street and started my usual. I asked about the dish written on the board. He described it, then looked towards the pan. I said I would have the new dish. He lowered the heat under the old one without asking. The new dish was good. I finished it, paid and stood up. There was a container waiting beside the till. “For later?” he asked. I took it. I am beginning to suspect that my attempts at spontaneity have a recognisable pattern.
