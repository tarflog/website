Frank needed a room emptied for repairs. I opened a dry space beside the landing, and we carried the furniture through. He asked how far back it went. I told him there was enough room. He marked out two aisles anyway. Boxes went on one side, furniture on the other, and the things he would need tomorrow stayed by the entrance. We finished before lunch. Nothing was lost, damp or unexpectedly alive. Frank called it a good morning’s work. Later he came to my door and held out his hand. I gave him back the marker. The labels on my sample cases are much easier to read now.
