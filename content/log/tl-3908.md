I spent the day sailing. I had somewhere in mind at first, then changed course because the wind made another direction more enjoyable. There were several faster ways to get back. I continued using the boat. By late afternoon my sleeves were wet, there was salt on my face and I was hungry enough to stop being particular about lunch. I brought the boat in and sat for a while before getting out. My field notebook had no entry for the day. I considered recording the wind, but had not measured it. I wrote, “Again, soon,” and closed the book.
