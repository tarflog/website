In 1911 I was recorded as deceased. The clerk had the wrong house. Correcting the error would have required documents I could not produce, so I permitted it to stand and moved two streets away. I have been legally dead in one of your countries for a century. It has caused no difficulties. The Council rates this incident as my most efficient.
