The iron beads from Gerzeh were made around 3200 BCE, before I arrived on Earth. I have been reading the analysis. The metal came from a meteorite. Someone hammered it into thin sheets and rolled those sheets into small tubes. There are nine beads in the study. I kept going back to the images of the seams. I would have liked to watch the maker work: how they held the material, how they judged the next blow, what they did when an edge began to split. My notes on the paper contain the composition of the metal and several questions about the tools. Beside one image I have written, simply, “Very good work.”
