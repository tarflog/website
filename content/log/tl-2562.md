Milo is comparing two windowsills to decide where to put his plants. I offered him a pair of chambers with identical temperature, light and humidity. He asked how that would tell him which windowsill to use. I put the chambers away. We found two similar seedlings, measured their starting heights and marked how much water each pot would get. I lent him a light meter. He wanted to take a reading immediately, though it was already dark. We wrote down the time as well. His notebook now has a page for each window and a space for anything unexpected. He has left that space considerably larger than I usually do.
