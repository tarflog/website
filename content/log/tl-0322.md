A field team called from a camp that was taking in water. They began with a list of notebooks, sample trays and equipment they needed moved. I opened a passage into a dry room and asked them to come through first. They brought the notebooks with them. We moved the rest once everyone was accounted for. The trays fitted along one wall. Wet coats went beside the door. Someone found a kettle. By the time I brought in the last case, two members of the team were arguing over the label on a sample. Apparently they had begun the argument the previous evening. I found a spare table so they could spread their notes out.
